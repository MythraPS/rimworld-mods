# RimWorld mods

- **IranianRugs** — Handwoven Rugs (for VFE - Art): Vanilla Furniture Expanded - Art's small, large and grand rugs as photos of 22 real antique carpets (public domain / CC0, sources in `Source/CARPET_SOURCES.md`), in their own colours, picked at random. With Ideology, Islamic and Christian styles get real prayer/saf carpets and an Armenian church carpet. `Source/make_rugs_photo.py` makes the textures, `Source/make_preview_photo.py` the Workshop preview, `Source/make_xml.py` the Ideology style defs.
- **JujutsuFundamentals** — Jujutsu Kaisen - Psycasts, Characters, Items, Quests (Workshop 3833457660): the sorcerers' techniques as Vanilla Psycasts Expanded paths, the characters, cursed tools and questlines in one mod, for RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7. It replaces the two mods below. C# in `Source` (build with `tools/build.sh`, which builds both game versions), defs in `Common`, art made by the `tools/` scripts.
- **GojoPsycasts** (old, merged into JujutsuFundamentals) — Satoru Gojo: his Limitless path for Vanilla Psycasts Expanded on an infinity-sign tree (Infinity, Blue, Red, Hollow Purple, Infinite Void, Fly and more), the Six Eyes, and Gojo himself arriving as a wanderer.
- **SukunaPsycasts** (old, merged into JujutsuFundamentals) — Ryomen Sukuna & Yuji Itadori: Sukuna's Shrine path for Vanilla Psycasts Expanded, unlocked by finding and consuming the twenty fingers through a questline, and Yuji Itadori, who joins colonies that earn his trust.
